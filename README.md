## Deep underground, your maps stay with you.

Route maps and station search live directly on your device. You can check your way even where cellular signal drops.

## Route search holds to release standards.

Navigation runs on our Journey V3 server. We do not return a route until every piece of required data and safety evidence is verified.

## Your situation sets the standard.

Wheelchair. Stroller. A temporary injury. The moment you choose your situation, the route changes. Step-free paths come first.

## We separate what we know from what we do not.

Every detail carries its source and the date it was checked. When we do not know, we say so directly.

## Report an issue without signing up.

If an elevator is out of service, report it on the spot. No account, no name needed. A single receipt number is enough.

## Nothing follows you.

No account. No tracking. No selling your habits. Your saved favorites never leave your phone.

## Current focus

We are validating accessibility details for Sangnoksu and Sadang stations, while preparing release evidence for the Gyeongchun Line ITX-Cheongchun route planner. The current release decision is NO_GO.
